# Westchester, New Jersey and Philadelphia, second pass, drafted on 27 November 2026

These 104 camps came from a second search of the three new regions, aimed at the towns, private camps and schools the first pass missed. Claude read each camp's own pages and wrote the draft by hand, every field tied to the page that states it, now with fees beyond tuition recorded from the start.

57 are ready and 47 need a decision from you. The branch adds 44 towns and townships to `campfinder/seed/cities.py` from the Census Bureau 2024 Gazetteer; postal towns the Census doesn't list are filed under the township its geocoder gives for the camp's address (for example Lafayette Hill under Springfield, Glen Mills under Thornbury, Mohegan Lake under Yorktown).
