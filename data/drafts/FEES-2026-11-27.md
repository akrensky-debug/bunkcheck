# Fees filled in on 27 November 2026

Andrew's rule of 26 November: costs beyond tuition go in a cost breakdown, not folded into the price. Claude re-read the camp's own pages for the 343 published camps whose listing mentioned a membership, fee, deposit, bus, lunch or extended care cost, and recorded each fee with its amount, unit and the page that states it.

- 176 camps now carry fees: 377 lines, 68 of them required (a membership, registration or activity fee). The rest are add-ons such as bus, extended care and lunch, shown on the camp page and left out of the plan total.
- 159 camps post no fee beyond tuition; 8 sites refused BunkcheckBot (Beaver, the three Trustees camps, two Greater Hartford YMCA camps, Roger Williams Park Zoo, URI Sailing Center) and 1 brochure was over the fetcher's 8 MB limit (Camp Whitin).
- 16 fees were dropped because the page prints no unit and the agent had picked one (Y STEAM and Danforth care, Renbrook care printed for another week, SoundWaters, North Star linens, Kennebec Explorers care, YMCA Camp of Maine laundry, Camp Bernadette, Camp Galil, Camp Ramsbottom care).
- Left out on purpose everywhere: deposits that go toward tuition, cancellation, late pickup and card fees, and any amount the page doesn't print.

## Questions for Andrew

1. **Per hour.** Tufts aftercare ($15), United Sports Arena care ($15), JCC Summer Days care ($30) and Far Brook extended day are priced by the hour, which isn't a unit yet. Add "hour"?
2. **One page per camp.** The validator takes fees from a single source page, so fees on a second page were left out (Wheeler's bus, Miquon's after care, ESF lunch at some campuses, Kettleford and Twin Hills bus, Newton's and Silvermine's registration fees). Allow each fee its own source?
3. **Charges that may sit inside the deposit.** Saginaw, Riverbend, ESF, Moosilauke, Waukeela, Hackley, Ashland and School's Out print a "registration" or "application" fee without saying whether it is on top of tuition. None were recorded.
4. **Listings due a 2027 refresh.** About ten camps listed for 2026 now print 2027 prices (Drumlin Farm, Broadmoor, Stony Brook, Crossroads Camp Wing, Cambridge YMCA, Clearbrook, Natick Farm, Franklin Rec, Camp Farnsworth); their 2027 add-ons were left out under the year rule.
5. **Girl Scout membership.** Kettleford, Seawood and Twin Hills record $80 a year from the council's join page, which also carries an older "$65 plus council fees" line.

Each camp's full notes are in the fee pass's working files, available on request.
