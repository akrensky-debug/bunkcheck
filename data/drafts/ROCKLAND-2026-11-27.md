# Rockland County: first camps, drafted on 27 November 2026

These 35 camps came from a search of Rockland's town and village recreation, the private day camps that bus families from New Jersey and Westchester, the Lewis JCC, schools, arts, sports and STEM camps. 14 sites refused BunkcheckBot. Claude read the rest from each camp's own pages with the repo fetcher only (no curl), and wrote each draft by hand with fees beyond tuition.

16 are ready and 19 need a decision from you. The branch adds `data/camps/rockland.json` (metro "Rockland County, NY") and 16 towns and hamlets to `campfinder/seed/cities.py` from the Census Bureau 2024 Gazetteer. Tomkins Cove is filed under Stony Point.
