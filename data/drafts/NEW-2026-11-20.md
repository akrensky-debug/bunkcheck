# New camps drafted on 20 November 2026

These 128 camps are new to Bunkcheck. They were found on town rec pages, YMCA branches, museums, schools, colleges and camp association directories, and checked against everything already published, drafted or tried. Claude read each camp's own pages with the repo fetcher (BunkcheckBot) and wrote the draft by hand. Every filled field names the page that states it, and each draft's `review.first_pass` says what's open.

49 are ready. 68 pass the validator but need a decision from you. 11 still lack a ZIP (decision 1). Weekly prices are the non-resident rate, per Andrew's rule of 22 November.

This branch also adds 49 towns to `campfinder/seed/cities.py`, so camps sit in their real town instead of a neighbor. Coordinates are each town's internal point from the Census Bureau 2024 Gazetteer (county subdivisions, which in New England are the towns).

## Promote the ready ones

Open a few and read their `first_pass`, then run promote with `--ready` for each folder, as in `READY-2026-11-20.md`, and `python -m campfinder.seed.import_real --check`.

## Decisions for you

1. **Decided 22 November: ZIP lookups are allowed.** Seven drafts now carry a ZIP from the Census Bureau geocoder, sourced to the query. 11 still fail: their camp pages give no street address (Camp Shriver, ACME Jr., Walpole, Camp Mansfield, Camp Carefree, Stratham, the Berwick Boys Foundation, Camp Laurel Wood, Pine Knoll), or the geocoder found none or two ZIPs (Camp Ingersoll, Edgerton Park).
2. **Residents only.** About ten town programs take only local residents (Bristol, Bonnet Shores, Manchester CT, Dover, Pelham, Stratham, Goffstown, Walpole and others). List them for the families who qualify, with a note, or leave them out?
3. **Academic summer schools.** Choate, Loomis Chaffee, Taft, Cardigan Mountain and Eaglebrook are selective academic programs. Loomis says it is "not summer school, not summer camp". List them as camps, as specialty, or not at all?
4. **Thin listings.** Some have a 2026 season but no dates or no prices (Biddeford Rec, Waterbury Rec, MALT, SEE Science Center, Worcester JCC, Greater Holyoke YMCA, Trinity Dream Camp). Publish what's posted, or wait for 2027?
5. **Two methods to approve.** Where a page gave only grades, some drafts derive ages from them and say so in the source note. Dunkley's Gymnastics posts its rates as a scanned PDF, which was downloaded once as BunkcheckBot and read as an image; one date on it is handwritten.

Smaller questions are in each draft's `for_andrew`. One worth knowing about: Camp Tecumseh's home page carries injected casino spam, a sign the site was hacked. The draft uses its rates page.
