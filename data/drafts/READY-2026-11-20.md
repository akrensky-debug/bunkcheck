# Drafts checked on 20 November 2026

These 39 camps failed the listing tool in October: a dead link, the wrong page, a site that was down, or a page that needed a browser. Claude found each camp's current page on its own site, read it with the repo fetcher (BunkcheckBot) and wrote the draft by hand. Every filled field names the page that states it. Each draft's `review.first_pass` says what changed and what's open.

18 are ready, 20 need a decision from you, and 1 is kept as a drop so the findings aren't lost.

## Decisions for you

1. **Resident or non-resident weekly price for town camps.** The research brief says non-resident, and these drafts follow it: Camp TREK (Merrimack), Camp Hamp, Camp KidZone and Safety Village (Northampton), and the three Greenwich camps. The published Camp Naticook, also Merrimack, uses the resident rate. One rule, and I'll make them match.
2. **Providence Recreation day camps.** The city runs ten center day camps with one schedule and price ($5 a week, 8:30 to 4, breakfast and lunch). No center has its own dates or ages. One citywide listing (needs a ZIP) or ten listings, one per center address? The city prints Vincent Brown's ZIP as 20906, a typo.
3. **A ZIP from outside the camp's own pages.** Camp Coney Pine (Rochester YMCA) and Camp Shalom (Windsor) publish no street address. Coney Pine's is 49 Lowell Street, 03867 on directories. Accept a ZIP lookup, or ask the camp?
4. **One listing or two.** Camp Mah-Kee-Nac: this draft is the whole camp, and the Mini Mak sessions are already published on their own. Indian Acres and Forest Acres: a boys camp and a girls camp, two Fryeburg addresses the site doesn't match up. Camp Fatima: keep the co-ed Special Needs Weeks inside the boys camp listing?
5. **Thin listings: publish now or wait for 2027.** Greenwood Day Camp (Longmeadow, no dates or prices), Springfield Boys & Girls Club (no ages or price), Mystic Aquarium (no prices), Camp Runels (sessions only in a flipbook guide).
6. **Smaller calls, each in its draft's `for_andrew`.** Camp Cedarwood publishes no ages. Camp Surefire's pages disagree on the start date (August 15 or 16). Centerboard sailing prices carry no year. Creative Kids Summer asks families to pay the teacher by check or Venmo. Wee Faerie Camp's first session is three days. Camp Parrot Perch prints Week 5 as Monday to Thursday.

Also: `data/drafts/boston/dedham-public-schools-summer-institute-dedham.json` was dropped on 12 November only because its page timed out. It loads now and every field matches. Change it to ready if you agree.
