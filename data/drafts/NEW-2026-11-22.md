# New camps, round two, drafted on 22 November 2026

These 61 camps came from a second search aimed at the gaps the first one left: town rec departments in larger towns, YMCA branches, Boys & Girls Clubs, museums, farms and overnight camps. Each one was checked against everything published, drafted or tried before. Claude read each camp's own pages with the repo fetcher (BunkcheckBot) and wrote the draft by hand, with every filled field tied to the page that states it.

35 are ready and 26 need a decision from you. Weekly prices are the non-resident rate, and ZIP codes missing from a camp's page were looked up with the Census geocoder where the camp gives a street address (both rules from 22 November).

This branch also adds 33 towns to `campfinder/seed/cities.py`, from the Census Bureau 2024 Gazetteer, as before.

## Ready to promote

Open a few and read their `first_pass`, then run `python -m campfinder.ingest.promote --ready data/drafts/<folder> --by "Andrew Krensky"` for each folder and `python -m campfinder.seed.import_real --check`.

- **Two sourcing calls:** East Hartford's and Brookfield's town sites refused BunkcheckBot; their drafts come from the towns' own MyRec registration sites, which did not. Children's Discovery Museum shows no year on its page, only on its 2026 registration forms.
