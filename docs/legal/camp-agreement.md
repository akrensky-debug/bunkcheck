## Part 1: Agreement text

### Bunkcheck Camp Agreement

This agreement is between **[Company]** ("Bunkcheck", "we") and the camp that accepts it ("the
camp", "you"). It covers two paid services, the verified check and paid inquiries, and the
listing content you send us. A camp can be listed on Bunkcheck and receive every parent's
inquiry without paying for anything.

#### 1. What you can buy

1.1 **The verified check.** We check your listing against your own documents and confirm it
with you. A listing that passes shows a verified mark for the season.

1.2 **Paid inquiries.** If you opt in, you pay for each parent inquiry we deliver to you that
is billable under section 4.

1.3 Neither service is required to be listed, to receive inquiries, or to appear in search.

#### 2. The verified check

2.1 **Price and payment.** The price is shown when you order (currently [$199] a season). We
charge it when you order, before we start the check.

2.2 **Season.** A check covers one season, 1 November to 31 August. A check bought partway
through a season ends on 31 August of that season.

2.3 **If we can't finish.** If we have not finished the check within 30 days of your payment,
we refund the full price. We may still finish the check later if you ask, at no charge.

2.4 **What the check is.** The check confirms that the listing matches what you gave us and
what we could confirm from your own published sources on the date of the check. It is not an
inspection, a license check, an accreditation, a safety review or an endorsement of your
program. We say so wherever the mark appears.

2.5 **When it ends early.** We may remove the verified mark, without refund, if you ask us to
show something you know is untrue, or if your listing changes in a way you don't confirm with
us within [14] days of our request.

#### 3. Paid inquiries

3.1 **Opting in.** You choose whether to pay for inquiries. You set a price at or below the
price we show you when you opt in (currently [$10] per billable inquiry) and a monthly cap.
You can change the cap or opt out at any time; a change applies to inquiries delivered after
we confirm it.

3.2 **Every inquiry is delivered.** We deliver every inquiry a parent confirms, whether or not
you opt in, whether or not you have reached your cap, and whether or not your invoices are
paid. Billing never decides which parent reaches you.

3.3 **Monthly cap.** Once billable inquiries in a calendar month reach your cap, further
inquiries that month are delivered and not billed.

#### 4. What counts as a billable inquiry

4.1 An inquiry is billable only if all of these are true:

- you had opted in when it was delivered;
- we delivered it to your verified contact address;
- it is the first billable inquiry from that family to your camp in the last 7 days;
- your monthly cap had not been reached; and
- we can tell which family sent it. An inquiry we can't attribute to one family is never
  billed.

4.2 Each invoice line shows the inquiry's date, its source (Bunkcheck, an AI assistant, or a
named partner app) and why it was billed.

#### 5. Disputes and credits

5.1 **Window.** You may flag a billed inquiry within 14 days of delivery. Flag it from the
inquiry page or by replying to the invoice email.

5.2 **Automatic credits.** We credit the first three flags in a calendar month automatically,
with no questions.

5.3 **Further flags.** We review any further flag in good faith and answer within [10]
business days. We credit an inquiry that is spam, a duplicate, sent by a vendor or competitor,
or clearly not from a parent or guardian.

5.4 A flag outside the 14-day window is not credited.

#### 6. Payment

6.1 **Method.** You pay by card or by bank transfer (ACH) through our payment processor,
Stripe. Stripe sends invoices and receipts on our behalf, with Bunkcheck branding.

6.2 **Inquiry invoices.** We invoice billable inquiries [monthly in arrears]. Payment is due
[14] days after the invoice date.

6.3 **Taxes.** Prices exclude taxes. We add any sales or use tax we are required to collect.

6.4 **Unpaid invoices.** If an invoice is unpaid [14] days after its due date:

- we keep delivering every inquiry;
- inquiries delivered while the invoice is unpaid are not billed, now or later; and
- your opt-in is paused until the invoice is paid. Paying it restarts billing for inquiries
  delivered after payment.

We may also refer the unpaid amount for collection. [Question for counsel: keep or drop.]

6.5 **Refunds.** The verified check is refunded only under section 2.3. Billed inquiries are
credited only under section 5.

#### 7. Ending the agreement

7.1 You may cancel at any time from your camp page or by email. Cancelling ends your opt-in at
once.

7.2 If you cancel after the verified check is done, there is no refund for the rest of the
season. The verified mark stays until 31 August unless section 2.5 applies.

7.3 If you cancel before the check is done, we refund the full price.

7.4 You still owe for inquiries billed before you cancelled.

7.5 We may end this agreement on [30] days' notice, or at once if you break section 8. If we
end it without cause, we refund the unused part of the season's check, pro rata by month.

#### 8. Parents' details

8.1 A parent decides what you receive. You get her question, and her name, email or phone
only if she chose to add them. You may get a link to an info kit package she made; it expires,
and she can withdraw it.

8.2 You use a parent's details only to answer her inquiry and, if she asks, to help her
register. You do not add her to a mailing list, sell or share her details, or use them for
marketing, unless she asks you to directly.

8.3 You keep a parent's details safe, with care at least equal to how you protect your own
families' records. You tell us within [72 hours] if you learn that details you got through
Bunkcheck were seen by someone who should not have seen them.

8.4 You delete a parent's details when she asks you to, unless you need them for a
registration she completed.

#### 9. Your listing content

9.1 **License.** You give us a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to copy, store,
reformat, translate, summarize and show the listing content you send us (text, dates, prices,
photos, brochures, logos) on Bunkcheck, in AI assistants that use Bunkcheck, and through
partner apps under our partner terms. The license lasts while you are listed and for [90] days
after, so that copies in partner apps can expire.

9.2 **Your promise.** You have the right to give us this content, and it does not infringe
anyone else's rights. You have permission from any person who appears in a photo you send.

9.3 **Our changes.** We may shorten, reformat or correct obvious errors in your content. We
keep a log of every change to your listing, with who made it and why, and show it to you on
request.

9.4 **Removal.** You may ask us to remove content at any time. We remove it from Bunkcheck
within [5] business days and tell partners to remove it.

9.5 Facts about your camp that are public (name, address, dates, prices) may stay on Bunkcheck
after you leave, marked as unverified and dated. [Question for counsel: see note 9.]

#### 10. Your program

10.1 You are responsible for your own program: staff, safety, supervision, licensing,
insurance, health care, refunds and everything you promise families.

10.2 Bunkcheck is not your agent and is not a party to any contract between you and a family.

#### 11. Ranking

11.1 Payment never changes ranking. Buying the verified check, opting into paid inquiries,
or the price you set does not move your camp up or down in any search, list, recommendation
or AI answer.

11.2 The verified mark is shown as a fact about the listing. It does not count as a ranking
signal. [Not true in code today: see Part 3, row 11.2.]

11.3 Our ranking rules are public at [link to the ranking rules page, item 7].

## Part 2: Notes for counsel

1. **Acceptance.** Camps will accept by checkbox at opt-in or checkout, often after a phone
   call. Is click acceptance enough for a business customer, and what record do we keep?
2. **Section 2.3, refund timer.** The 30 days run from payment. Should a camp's own delay
   (not sending documents) pause the clock?
3. **Section 2.4, what the check is.** Parents may read "verified" as a safety claim. Is the
   disclaimer here and on the mark enough, or should the mark be renamed? Does any state
   regulate the word "verified" for youth programs?
4. **Section 3.1, price set by the camp.** The brief says "the camp's opted-in price". The code
   records one price for everyone (`INQUIRY_PRICE_CENTS`, default 1000). Is the price ours or
   the camp's? The draft reads it as ours, with the camp free to accept it or not.
5. **Section 4, the 7-day rule.** We identify a family by a hash of its account, partner user
   reference or email. A family using two assistants may count twice. Is "family" defined
   tightly enough for billing?
6. **Section 5.2, automatic credits.** Should credits be capped in dollars as well as count?
7. **Section 6.4, unpaid invoices.** We waive billing for inquiries delivered while unpaid.
   Does that create any problem collecting the earlier invoice?
8. **Section 8, parents' details.** Does the camp become our service provider, a third party,
   or an independent controller for the parent's details? This drives whether a paid inquiry
   is a "sale" (item 17). The draft limits the camp's use as tightly as possible.
9. **Section 9.5, public facts after a camp leaves.** Are we free to keep showing public facts
   about a camp that ended the agreement? This overlaps the camp data sourcing memo (item 16).
10. **Section 11, ranking.** Does a paid verified mark shown in results need a disclosure
    under FTC endorsement or advertising guidance, even though it does not change rank?
11. **Minors.** Inquiries are about children. Is any extra term needed for a camp that receives
    a child's details through an info kit package?
12. **Tax.** Section 6.3 assumes the accountant's answer on item 20.
