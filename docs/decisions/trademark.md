## Clearance

- BUNK-CHECK (serial 74431016, Micro Chemical, Inc., 1993, Class 9 feedyard software) is
  **dead**: abandoned, no Statement of Use filed. Not a bar.
- Risk: "bunk check" is camp slang (e.g. a camp's bunk-availability page at `/bunkcheck`).
  The examiner may call it merely descriptive (Section 2(e)(1)). Our answer is that it is
  suggestive: it evokes camp without describing search, comparison or planning. Always use it
  as one word, BunkCheck.
- Before filing, search tmsearch.uspto.gov for BUNKCHECK, BUNK CHECK, BUNKCHEK and similar
  marks in Classes 9, 35, 41 and 42, and note the date and result here.
- Consider an hour with a trademark attorney because of the descriptiveness risk.

8. [ ] Ignore payment solicitations after filing; real USPTO mail comes from `@uspto.gov`.
